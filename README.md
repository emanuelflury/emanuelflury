### How I work

- **Finance first, then code.** Years of supply chain finance, transfer pricing and finance automation taught me where the real work hides: in exports, reconciliations and month-end close.
- **Automate the boring, keep the judgement.** I design AI agents and workflows that take over repetitive steps, so people can focus on decisions.
- **Lean and open.** Self-hosted language models, open-source building blocks and clear documentation over black boxes.
